## Task

Design a premium digital experience for UAcoustics, a brand creating architectural acoustic panels. The goal was to position their products not just as technical soundproofing, but as high-end interior design pieces deeply tied to psychological well-being and biophilic design.

## Problem

Acoustic panels are traditionally perceived as purely functional, industrial, and visually unappealing. The challenge was to completely shift this perception, translating the abstract concept of "listening to silence" into a highly visual, luxurious, and calming digital space, while keeping the product catalog easy to navigate.

## Solution

I went with a clean, magazine-style layout, using elegant fonts and plenty of white space to give the site a calm, breathable vibe. To really make the products stand out, I used 3D renderings that make the acoustic panels look like modern architecture. Paired with a soft, natural color palette and a sleek project gallery, the site turns a highly technical product into a premium design experience.
