## Task

A newly established counselor needed a complete visual identity and social media system from scratch. The goal was to build a warm, authentic online presence that could tackle complex emotional topics gently and naturally.

## Problem

The client was inspired by highly intricate, ever-changing social media profiles. My challenge was managing these high expectations while honestly explaining that complex visual variety requires immense time and advanced design skills. I had to guide her toward understanding that, for a solo professional, a consistent and easily reproducible style is actually much more effective for building brand recognition.

## Solution

We created an intimate, handwritten aesthetic mimicking a dotted notebook, using a signature yellow accent to highlight text and frame her own custom doodles. To guarantee her long-term success, I delivered a comprehensive brand guideline (detailing exact sizes, pairings, and technical specs), giving her a deeply personal system she could easily maintain without burning out.
