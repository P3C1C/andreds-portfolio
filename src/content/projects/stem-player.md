## Task

I was asked to design the interface for an experimental web-based Stem Player, a tool that isolates vocals and instruments in a track. The goal was to create a modern, minimal digital tool that actually felt like a tangible piece of hardware you could hold in your hands.

## Problem

I didn’t have a background in audio engineering, so the main challenge was understanding the mechanics of a stem player first. I needed to take a complex set of audio controls and turn them into an intuitive interface that anyone could pick up and use immediately, without feeling overwhelmed.

## Solution

By teaming up closely with the developer, I bridged the gap between technical complexity and intuitive design. I used realistic shadows and metallic finishes to create a highly tactile UI. With physical-looking buttons, knobs for effects (like distortion and echo), and clear sliders for individual instruments, we turned an advanced audio tool into an accessible, playful, and visually satisfying experience.
