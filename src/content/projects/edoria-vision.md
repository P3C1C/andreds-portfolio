## Task

Two young ophthalmologists wanted to elevate their practice and attract a specialized niche of clients. Since it was their first time working with a designer, my job wasn't just to create a logo, social assets, and informative PDFs. I also guided them through the entire creative workflow, setting clear expectations, timelines, and communication boundaries.

## Problem

The biggest challenge was longevity. They needed to manage their own communication after the project ended, without any graphic design background. On top of that, we needed to explain complex medical treatments, risks and benefits included, in a way that felt accessible, rather than cold and institutional.

## Solution

We broke the rules of traditional healthcare branding. I designed a modern-vintage identity featuring friendly animal photos (cats and dogs!) to explain medical dynamics with a smile. To ensure their complete autonomy, I delivered a comprehensive, easy-to-follow brand guideline PDF and focused our social media strategy on Instagram Reel covers, giving them a practical, low-barrier way to create content independently.
