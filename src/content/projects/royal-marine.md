## Task

Design a premium, mobile-first website for a Rimini-based company launching an innovative line of floating docks for small boats. The goal was to reflect the heavy investment behind this new product with a custom-tailored digital experience that felt distinctly Italian, warm, and deeply connected to the sea.

## Problem

The client was a large, busy company, which meant direct communication and feedback loops were very limited. I had to work highly independently, finding a way to hit their high expectations and move the project forward without relying on constant check-ins.

## Solution

I took a proactive approach, preparing strong, well-thought-out options to respect the client’s time and earn their trust. To highlight the product's uniqueness, I integrated the actual physical shape of the floats as a recurring custom UI element throughout the site. By collaborating closely with the web developer, we delivered a polished, cohesive mobile experience that beautifully reflects the quality of their project.
