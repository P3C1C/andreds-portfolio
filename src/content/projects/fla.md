## Task

Design a comprehensive website for Future Lab Academy, a platform built to promote open-source IT skills and digital transition. The goal was to create a digital hub that connects companies, public institutions, and students through training paths and collaborative spaces.

## Problem

I had to communicate highly technical and institutional concepts without making the site feel like a dense, bureaucratic portal. The design needed enough authority to reassure high-level partners, but it also had to feel modern and accessible to engage a younger audience. The biggest hurdle was organizing a massive amount of text without overwhelming the user.

## Solution

I went with an elegant, dark navy aesthetic to instantly project professionalism and innovation. To balance the academic feel with a tech-forward vibe, I paired a refined serif font with technical graphic elements like thin lines and 3D wireframes. Finally, to make the dense information digestible, I built clean, modular grid layouts with numbered sections, turning complex text blocks into a highly scannable and visually pleasing experience.
