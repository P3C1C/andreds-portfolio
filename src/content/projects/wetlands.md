## Task

I was asked to design the website for Wetlands, a new independent, eco-sustainable publishing house based in Venice. They needed a digital home to showcase their unique book collections (like Barene, Mude and Fondamenta) and highlight their strong commitment to social and environmental sustainability.

## Problem

The challenge was figuring out how to make a digital platform feel organic and eco-friendly without falling into the usual "green leaves" clichés. The site had to feel like a proper, authoritative editorial space, while also acting as the perfect frame for their modern, minimalist book covers, which feature solid colors and soft geometric shapes.

## Solution

To give the interface a physical, tactile vibe, I used a textured background that looks exactly like raw, recycled paper. I paired this organic feel with elegant serif fonts to keep that classic publishing authority intact. By keeping the layout super clean and minimal, the design lets their vibrant book covers do the talking, creating a browsing experience that feels authentic and perfectly aligned with their sustainable mission.
