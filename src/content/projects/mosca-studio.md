## Task

Design a social media identity (carousels, Reel covers) and informative PDFs for a couple of dentists. The goal was to showcase their services and methodology while accurately capturing their genuinely warm, caring, and gentle approach to patient care.

## Problem

Dentistry branding is traditionally cold, clinical, and often anxiety-inducing. The main challenge was breaking this mold to visually reflect their welcoming nature and attract patients looking for exactly that kind of reassurance. Additionally, as first-time design clients who needed future autonomy, finding the sweet spot between a highly professional look and an easy-to-edit system was crucial.

## Solution

Because they were incredibly trusting but unsure of their visual direction, I guided them through a series of key strategic questions to pinpoint their ideal aesthetic. We explored multiple concepts together before landing on a warm, inviting visual identity. I then built a simplified, reusable design system that they still use today with complete independence and confidence.
