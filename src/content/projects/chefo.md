## Task

I designed Chefo, an app created to help everyday people improve their diet quality, manage their time in the kitchen, and reduce food waste. The goal was to build a highly intuitive platform for a diverse audience (from busy single moms to retirees) who lack advanced cooking skills and need a simple, stress-free digital tool.

## Problem

Cooking three times a day is a major frustration due to lack of time and ideas, often forcing users to rely on expensive ready-made meals or delivery. This leads to repetitive diets, unhealthy eating habits, and a lot of food going to waste in the fridge. Competitor research revealed that existing apps are either just disconnected grocery lists (lacking integration) or overly complex recipe hubs cluttered with ads and poor navigation.

## Solution

I designed a unified user experience that solves all these pain points in one place. The core of the app is a clean "Weekly Planner" that allows users to schedule meals in advance, ensuring dietary variety. To eliminate friction, I integrated a dynamic grocery list that syncs seamlessly with the meal plan. Featuring a soft, friendly UI and quick search filters (by time, difficulty, and cooking method), Chefo turns finding easy recipes and managing groceries into a relaxing process rather than a daily chore.
