## Task

I handled the end-to-end UI/UX design and branding for Kanpai, a social app created to connect people through shared activities and passions. The goal was to build, in just three weeks, an intuitive and accessible platform for a diverse audience (ages 20 to 50), fostering shared experiences and authentic relationships.

## Problem

The main challenge was overcoming the natural hesitation of meeting strangers on the internet, which can often feel risky and intimidating. It was essential to build a digital space that felt genuinely safe and transparent. Plus, since the app relies entirely on community-generated events, the content creation process had to be completely frictionless, ensuring new users wouldn't feel overwhelmed.

## Solution

I structured the user experience around social proof, making group chats the core feature of the app. This choice not only makes organizing easier but naturally exposes and discourages bad behavior, which is less likely to happen in a group setting. Visually, I went with a bright, colorful, and welcoming design, supported by smooth, swipe-based navigation. To encourage active participation, I designed the event creation flow using a clean, vertical modular layout, guiding the user step-by-step in a relaxed, easy-to-digest way.
